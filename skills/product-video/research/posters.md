## Patterns

- **Headline length runs 2–9 words, clustering at 4 and 6.** Of 66 App Store panel headlines transcribed above, the mode is 4 words (`appstore-1blocker-01-ipad.png`, `appstore-clover-go-02-ipad.png`, `appstore-loyverse-pos-02-ipad.png`) and second mode is 6 words (`appstore-square-pos-02-ipad.png` "Enable Tap to Pay on iPhone.", `appstore-toast-now-01-iphone.png`); the longest are POS benefit statements at 8–9 words (`appstore-clover-go-03-iphone.png` "View open orders and closed orders in real time", `appstore-paste-03-iphone.png` "Find what you copied just now or long ago"). Nothing in the sample ran past 9 words.
- **Single-device panels dominate; multi-device panels are reserved for "ecosystem" claims.** Most POS and ad-blocker panels (`appstore-eatos-*`, `appstore-wipr2-*`, `appstore-loyverse-*`) show exactly one phone or tablet. Multi-device compositions (2–3 devices fanned out) appear only where the pitch is cross-platform reach: `appstore-paste-01-ipad.png` (Mac+iPad+iPhone, "A Better Way to Copy and Paste") and `appstore-altos-pos-01-ipad.png` (tablet+phone, "A single app for your entire business").
- **Solid-color full-bleed backgrounds are the default for App Store panels; photography is reserved for lifestyle/trust framing.** Flat color fills (`appstore-clover-go-01-ipad.png` green, `appstore-loyverse-pos-01-ipad.png` dark green, `appstore-eatos-01-ipad.png` magenta, `appstore-square-pos-01-ipad.png` black, `appstore-wipr2-01-ipad.png` purple gradient) vastly outnumber photographic backgrounds, which show up only for "in a real business" claims (`appstore-square-retail-01-ipad.png`, `square-square-01-hero-desktop.png`, `square-square-03-feature-grow.png`).
- **The device, when present, is oversized relative to the canvas — 65–90% of frame height** — not a small inset. Compare this against the one panel that does the opposite, `appstore-1blocker-01-ipad.png` and `-02-ipad.png`, which use a small inset phone precisely because that panel is a trust/badge slide, not a feature slide.
- **Product Hunt gallery images are raw, unstyled screenshots — no added headline, no color-block background.** All 67 kept `producthunt-*` files are literal window/screen captures (menu-bar drop-downs, macOS app windows, iOS screens) with the OS's own chrome as the only "background." Use these for authentic UI-chrome and cursor/interaction detail, not for hero-panel typography reference.
- **Caption position on marketing (non-App-Store) panels sits at the top of the frame, left- or center-aligned, above the device — never overlaid on top of the screenshot itself.** True in every transcribed App Store panel above and in the two Qasir/Square feature crops that include visible copy.
- **Competitor POS hero images mix two registers: hardware product photography and lifestyle-with-device-cutout.** Square supplies both isolated hardware renders on plain backgrounds (`square-square-05-hardware-terminal.png`, `-06-hardware-register.png`, `-07-hardware-kiosk.png`) and warm lifestyle photos with a screenshot composited in (`square-square-01-hero-desktop.png`, `qasir-qasir-01-hero.webp`, `loyverse-loyverse-01-hero.webp`) — no plain color-block treatment appears in this competitor set at all, unlike the App Store panels.
- **Gated/skipped sources, recorded rather than substituted:** `pos.toasttab.com` (Toast's marketing site) returned HTTP 403 to a direct fetch and was not opened in a browser to work around it — skipped, not fabricated. Majoo's marketing homepage (`majoo.id`) loaded but exposed no usable hero/feature image beyond small case-study thumbnails and Play Store badges — skipped for lack of a poster-worthy asset, not gated.
