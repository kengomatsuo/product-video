# Netflix Partner Help Center — Timed Text Style Guide: Subtitle Timing Guidelines

## Notes

- **Minimum subtitle duration**: 5/6 of a second (20 frames at 24fps) — nothing shorter is allowed to appear on screen.
- **Maximum subtitle duration**: 7 seconds per subtitle event.
- **Minimum gap between subtitles**: 2 frames, at any frame rate.
- At 24fps specifically: any gap of 3–11 frames must be closed down to exactly 2 frames (i.e., no "almost touching but not quite" gaps — either essentially butted or a deliberate half-second-plus break).
- **Reading speed (characters per second)**: 20 cps max for adult content, 17 cps max for children's content (figures vary slightly by target language — e.g. French adult content is capped lower, at 17 cps, 13 cps for kids).

## Quote (under 15 words)

"Subtitles should not be any shorter in duration than 20 frames."
