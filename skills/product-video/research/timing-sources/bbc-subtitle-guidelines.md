# BBC Subtitle Guidelines — Reading Speed & Minimum Duration

URL: https://www.clevercast.com/bbc-subtitling-guidelines/ (summary of the BBC's published subtitle guidelines; the BBC's own guidelines document is not reliably reachable outside partner access)

Fetched: 2026-09-24 (WebSearch aggregation)

## Notes

- **Recommended reading speed**: 160–180 words per minute, i.e. roughly **0.33–0.375 seconds per word**.
- **Minimum on-screen duration**: about 0.3 seconds per word as a floor (example given: a 4-word subtitle needs at least ~1.2 seconds).
- The BBC is explicit that timing is ultimately an editorial judgment call layered on top of the numeric floor — actual speech speed, on-screen text elsewhere, and shot changes all push the real number around.
- BBC's numbers are noticeably more generous (slower) than Netflix's 20 cps ceiling — worth noting when a source disagrees: BBC is tuned for a broad, older, general-audience broadcast viewer; Netflix's cps model is tuned per-language and skews faster for younger/adult content.
