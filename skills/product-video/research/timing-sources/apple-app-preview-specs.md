# Apple App Store Connect — App Preview Video Specifications

URL: https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/app-preview-specifications

Fetched: 2026-09-24 (WebFetch, direct)

## Notes

- **Length**: minimum 15 seconds, maximum 30 seconds per App Preview.
- **Count**: up to 3 App Previews can be delivered per app, per supported language/locale.
- **File size cap**: 500 MB. **Frame rate cap**: 30 fps. **Target bitrate** (H.264): 10–12 Mbps.
- **Poster frame**: defaults to the frame at 5 seconds into the preview unless a different poster frame is chosen — meaning the first 5 seconds carry extra weight since that's what a browsing user sees as the static thumbnail before tapping play.
- Apple doesn't mandate seconds-per-feature, but the 15–30 s hard ceiling means a preview covering 2–3 features has roughly **5–10 seconds per feature** to spare if it also needs an open and a close.

## Quote (under 15 words)

"App previews can be between 15 and 30 seconds long."
