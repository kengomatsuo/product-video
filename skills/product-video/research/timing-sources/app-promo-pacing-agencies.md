## Notes

- **Overall length bands** by use case: web/homepage explainer demos run **45–120 s**; social/feature-announcement "micro-demos" run **10–30 s** and focus on one capability.
- **Shot pacing inside a demo**: agencies report averaging **3–4 seconds per shot** for a "careful, exploratory" feel matching someone actually navigating the app — faster than that starts to feel like a highlight reel rather than a walkthrough.
- **UI screen time inside a longer spot**: even in a 60-second commercial, the actual app UI may only be on screen for a total of **8–10 seconds**, appearing as brief cutaways rather than a sustained screen recording — the emotional/lifestyle footage carries the rest.
- **Opening hook**: the first **3 seconds** are treated as make-or-break for retention (cited figure: ~71% of overall retention is decided in that window), so the core value proposition needs to land immediately, before any logo or scene-setting.
- **Homepage recommendation**: a **60–90 s** outcome-led overview, ending on a clear call to action (trial/demo).

## Quote (under 15 words)

"pacing averages 3-4 seconds per shot"
