# Social Video Caption Duration — TikTok / Creator Practice

URL: https://www.opus.pro/blog/tiktok-caption-subtitle-best-practices

Fetched: 2026-09-24 (WebSearch; Meta and YouTube's own creator docs don't publish a specific caption-duration number, so this is creator-community best practice rather than a platform spec)

## Notes

- Common creator-side rule: keep short on-screen text/captions visible for **2–3 seconds minimum**, long enough to read twice, matching the general "read aloud twice" rule above.
- If more text is packed on screen than fits that window, best practice is to shorten the copy rather than extend the hold — vertical, sound-off, thumb-scrolling viewers won't wait.
- Platform-native tools (TikTok's caption "Set duration" control) let creators trim exactly when text appears/disappears — the trend is toward tighter per-word or per-line timing (word-by-word karaoke captions) rather than one long static caption block, because per-word reveal reads faster than a full static line at the same total duration.
- No official Meta or YouTube numeric spec was found in this research pass; this is inferred from creator tooling and community guidance only — flagged as lower-confidence than the Netflix/BBC broadcast numbers.

## Quote (under 15 words)

"leave text on screen long enough to read it twice"
