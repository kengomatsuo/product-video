# "Read It Aloud Twice" Rule — On-Screen Title Duration

URL: https://www.ssw.com.au/rules/post-production-do-you-give-enough-time-to-read-texts-in-your-videos

Fetched: 2026-09-24 (WebSearch)

## Notes

- Practical, widely-repeated rule of thumb among editors and motion designers (not from a single named authority): hold any on-screen title/caption long enough that a slow reader could read it through **twice** before it's gone.
- Baseline reading speed used for the calculation: ~200 words per minute for a "slow reader," so duration = (word count / 200 wpm) × 60 seconds × 2.
- Explicitly framed as a floor, not a target — the source notes it can be excessive for very short titles and too fast for dense text, so it's a sanity check rather than a formula to apply blindly.
- Functions as the plain-language version of the more rigorous BBC/Netflix cps rules above, aimed at editors without a style guide to hand.

## Quote (under 15 words)

"leaving them on screen long enough... to finish reading it at least twice"
