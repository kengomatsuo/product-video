## Cross-video patterns

- **Kinetic-type title cards on solid black are the default scene transition.** Linear uses them constantly (linear-26, linear-38, linear-46, linear-58); Things uses the same move with color instead of pure black (things-80, things-82, things-85).
- **Orbiting thin-ring diagrams are Linear's signature motif for anything agent/automation-related** (linear-42 through linear-45, linear-52 through linear-57) — a single hairline circle with tick marks, small dot nodes, and text labels that appear as the dot passes them, always ending on a plain wordmark card (linear-46, linear-58).
- **End cards are minimal and near-identical within a brand**: small centered logomark or wordmark on a near-black or plain background, held for roughly a full second (linear-36, linear-40b, linear-51, linear-58, notion end frames absent since Notion clips loop rather than end-card, things-86 white background variant).
- **Cursor is drawn as a plain OS-style arrow, never a custom graphic**, and its motion is the main way a demo signals "this is a live product": Notion's board drag (notion-59/60), Linear's modal click (linear-33/34), Screen Studio's spreadsheet drag (screenstudio-75/76).
- **Screen recordings pair a circular webcam bubble with an auto-captioned subtitle bar** (screenstudio-74/75/76) — the caption text changes per frame and always sits bottom-center, bubble bottom-left.
- **3D-tilted device mockups (perspective/parallax) signal "mobile" sections**, flattening back to a straight-on mockup for close-up detail shots in the same sequence (things-78/83 tilted vs. things-81/84 flat; linear-47/48/49 tilted phone).
- **Split/paired-panel comparisons show a "before" (empty or loading) state next to an "after" (populated) state** to sell AI generation speed: Notion's bug tracker (notion-64→66), enterprise search (notion-70→71), and meeting notes (notion-72→73) all use this loading-to-resolved pairing.
- **Talking-head keynote footage is graded near-monochrome/dark and intercut with brief (under 1s) UI cutaways** rather than long screen-capture segments: Linear's launch video alternates speaker shots (linear-16/17/19/20/21/23) with quick product inserts (linear-18/22/24/25) every few seconds.
